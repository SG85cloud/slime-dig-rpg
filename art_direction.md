# Art Direction - Slime Mine RPG

- **Visual Style**: 16-bit isometric aesthetic using stylized 3D models. Low-poly with clean, vibrant textures that mimic pixel art.
- **Palette**: Deep gothic underground (dark purples, grays) contrasted by glowing ores (Cyan Mithril, Gold, Rusty Iron, Coal).
- **Key Assets**:
    - **Models**: Leader Slime (heroic blue), Worker Slime (servant green), Coal Ore (dark rock), Iron Ore (rusty lump), Gold Ore (sparkling nugget), Mithril Crystal (glowing cyan).
    - **Textures**: Cracked stone floor tiles, cave wall textures.
    - **UI**: Pixel-style icons for ore types and slime traits.
- **Lighting**: Dappled torchlight effects, glowing crystals.
