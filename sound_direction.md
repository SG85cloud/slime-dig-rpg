# Sound Direction - Slime Mine RPG

- **Music**: Atmospheric, loopable dungeon-synth track. Mysterious and slightly tense, suitable for a gothic catacomb.
- **SFX**:
    - **Mining**: Sharp metallic 'tink' and crumbling rock sounds.
    - **Combat**: Wet squishing hits for slime combat.
    - **Interaction**: Satisfying inventory chime for ore pickups.
    - **UI**: Short, retro-style clicks and hover sounds.
- **Playback**: First-gesture unlock; volume slider in UI.
